439862-1 St. Johns Loop - U.S. 1 at Kennedy Avenue to Dale Street

Work Type: Traffic Study
Phase: PD&E
Length: 13 miles
County:
  • Volusia
Road:
  • US 1

The St. Johns Loop Project Development and Environment (PD&E) Study is being conducted to develop and evaluate options for a multi-use trail from U.S. 1 at Kennedy Parkway in unincorporated Volusia County to Dale Avenue in the City of Edgewater, a distance of approximately 13 miles. The PD&E study started in May 2017 and is anticipated to last approximately one year.

The objective of this study is to identify a route where a 12-foot, multi-use trail option can connect from the planned trail along U.S. 1 to Dale Street in the City of Edgewater. This project is part of a greater effort to close the gaps and complete the St. Johns Loop.
