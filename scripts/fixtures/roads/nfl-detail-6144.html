Overview

Ponce Associates, LLC has requested a permit to construct a traffic signal on U.S. 1 and Camacha Park Drive in St. Augustine for future background growth and proposed developments in the area.

To maintain safety and manage access to the proposed site, FDOT would require the developer to construct a traffic signal on U.S. 1 and Camacha Park Drive. The developer would fund this construction at no cost to taxpayers.

If you have questions or comments about the proposed traffic signal, please contact Permits Manager Claire Galloway, at (904) 825-5026, Claire.Galloway@dot.state.fl.us or by mail at 3600 DOT Rd., St. Augustine, FL 32084.
