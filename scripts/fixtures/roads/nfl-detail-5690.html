Coastal Highway from SR A1A to 24th Street drainage improvements & shared use path construction
Project Start:Winter 2028
Expected Completion:NA
Cost:$8.5 million
Project #:429931-1, 429931-2
RoadsState Road A1A
CountiesSt. Johns
CitiesPonte Vedra
Overview

This project proposes improvements on Coastal Highway (State Road A1A) from the Jimmy Buffett Memorial Highway (State Road A1A) intersection to 24th Street. This project will increase safety for motorists, pedestrians, bicyclists, children and other users of the corridor.

FDOT proposes the following improvements:

  • An 8-foot-wide trail for pedestrians and cyclists
  • Minor restriping for three left-turn lanes in the northbound direction
    • Two right-turn southbound lanes will be removed.
  • Drainage improvements in various locations to improve roadway flooding
